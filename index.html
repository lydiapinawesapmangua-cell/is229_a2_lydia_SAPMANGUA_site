How a ticket moves through the helpdesk

Every request follows the same four stages, so you always know where your issue stands.

  1. Submit

    Log your issue through the ticket form with as much detail as you can. You'll receive a ticket number by email immediately.

  2. Triage

    A Tier 1 technician reviews the ticket, confirms priority and either resolves it directly or assigns it to a specialist.

  3. Resolve

    The assigned technician works the ticket, contacting you if more information or remote access is needed.

  4. Confirm

    We close the ticket once you confirm the issue is fixed, and ask for a quick rating to help us improve.
