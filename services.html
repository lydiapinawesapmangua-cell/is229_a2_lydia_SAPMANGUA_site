Services

The helpdesk covers six areas of support. Each ticket you submit is automatically routed to the technicians who specialise in that category, using the category you choose on the ticket form.

What we support

Tier 1–2

Hardware support

Laptops, desktops, monitors, docking stations, printers and peripherals. Includes loan equipment while a repair is in progress.

Tier 1–2

Software support

Installation, licensing, updates and troubleshooting for both Pinawe's internal tools and standard office software.

Tier 2

Network & connectivity

Office Wi-Fi, wired connections, VPN access for remote work, and connectivity for meeting rooms.

Tier 1

Account & access management

New starter accounts, password resets, permission changes, and account deactivation for departing staff.

Tier 3

Security & incident response

Reporting phishing attempts, suspected compromise, lost devices, and general security advice.

Tier 1–2

Training & onboarding

Short walkthroughs for new tools, onboarding setup for new starters, and refresher sessions on request.

Service scope and limits

To keep response times fair for everyone, the helpdesk supports company-owned devices and approved software only. A small number of requests fall outside standard support.

In-scope versus out-of-scope requests
In scope Out of scope
Company-issued laptops and phones Personal devices, unless enrolled in Bring Your Own Device
Approved software from the company catalogue Unlicensed or unapproved third-party software
Office and approved remote-work network access Personal home network configuration

Requests outside this scope can still be logged — we'll advise on the best next step, such as a supported alternative.
