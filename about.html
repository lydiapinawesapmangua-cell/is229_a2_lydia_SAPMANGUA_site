About the Pinawe IT Helpdesk

The Pinawe IT Helpdesk is the internal technology support team of Pinawe Software Co. We exist so that staff across engineering, sales, finance and operations — and Pinawe's registered software clients — can get technology problems solved quickly, without needing to understand the systems behind the scenes themselves.

Our mission

We aim to remove technology friction from people's working day. That means treating every ticket as a real interruption to someone's work, communicating clearly in plain language, and closing the loop once a problem is actually fixed — not just marked resolved in a system.

  • Respond to every ticket within its published target, every time.
  • Explain fixes in language a non-technical staff member can follow.
  • Keep a public record of known issues so people aren't left guessing.
  • Reduce repeat tickets by publishing self-service guides in the knowledge base.

How the team is structured

Tickets are handled by the tier best suited to the issue, so straightforward requests never wait behind complex ones.

Support tiers and their responsibilities
Tier Handles Typical examples
Tier 1 — Service Desk First contact, password resets, account access, basic troubleshooting Locked account, printer not responding, software install request
Tier 2 — Technical Support Hardware repairs, application errors, network connectivity Laptop won't boot, VPN drops repeatedly, application crash
Tier 3 — Systems & Security Infrastructure, servers, security incidents, escalations Server outage, suspected phishing compromise, data recovery

What guides how we work

Clarity over jargon

Every ticket update explains what happened and what happens next, without assuming technical background.

Security by default

We verify identity before making account changes and never ask for a password over email or phone.

Fix it once

Recurring issues get root-caused and documented, rather than resolved the same way every time.
